Classroom cognitive experiment

Shape Sentinel

A simple task designed to study prospective memory — remembering to carry out an intended action when a particular event happens.

Participant task: Identify the shape shown in the centre of the screen. Also remember to press 1 whenever the central shape is a blue triangle, as well as the triangle key.

Participant details

Instructions

1. Identify the shape

F = circle

J = square

K = triangle

2. Remember the special rule

Whenever the central shape is a blue triangle, press 1 as well as K.

There are two rounds. In one round, only the central shape appears. In the other, four coloured shapes appear around it. Ignore the surrounding shapes and respond only to the central coloured target. The order of the rounds is alternated by participant ID.

Timing: Each shape appears briefly. The complete task takes about 4–6 minutes, including practice.
Researcher: central data storage

Completed sessions are sent securely to the study database. At the end of collection, download the participant summary, trial data, and separate contact table from the Supabase SQL Editor.
